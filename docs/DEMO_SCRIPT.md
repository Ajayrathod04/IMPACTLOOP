# ImpactLoop Competition Demo Script (3–5 Minutes)

## Objective
Demonstrate ImpactLoop to hackathon judges in a tight, compelling 3-5 minute narrative highlighting innovation, UX, problem solving, and technical execution.

---

### 0:00 – Problem Statement & Positioning
- **Visual**: Landing Page (`LandingExperience`).
- **Narrative**:
  > "Product changes rarely fail at the single point of edit. They fail because of unexpected downstream consequences across product, support, billing, and revenue systems. ImpactLoop is AI Change-Impact Decision Intelligence that lets teams see what their change sets in motion before they ship."

---

### 0:30 – Enter Spatial Decision Observatory
- **Action**: Click `[ EXPLORE THE IMPACT ]`.
- **Visual**: Seamless camera transition into 3D Spatial Decision Observatory loaded with canonical demo `Free Trial: 14 days → 7 days`.
- **Narrative**:
  > "We are looking at our spatial decision observatory. At the center is our proposed change: shortening our free trial from 14 days to 7 days. Orbiting spatially around it are all downstream systems."

---

### 1:00 – Replay Causal Impact Propagation
- **Action**: Click `[ REPLAY IMPACT ]`.
- **Visual**: Glowing pulse travels from Change Core → SIGNUP → ONBOARDING → ACTIVATION → SUPPORT → REVENUE.
- **Narrative**:
  > "When we click Replay Impact, ImpactLoop animates the causal propagation signal travelling through our system nodes in real time."

---

### 1:30 – Inspect Affected Systems & Provenance
- **Action**: Click `SUPPORT QUEUE` node.
- **Visual**: `ImpactInspector` opens showing Severity 84/100, Why Affected (+34% surge in Day 5-6 tickets), and linked telemetry evidence.
- **Narrative**:
  > "Clicking any node opens the contextual inspector. Notice our provenance tags: ImpactLoop doesn't guess; it labels facts, probabilistic inferences, and explicit unknowns."

---

### 2:15 – Evidence Vault & Explicit Unknowns
- **Action**: Click `[ UNKNOWNS ]` tab in spatial HUD.
- **Visual**: `UnknownsPanel` surfaces "Impact on Annual Plan Conversion Rate" with resolution method "Run cohort comparison across acquisition channels".
- **Narrative**:
  > "Crucially, ImpactLoop treats uncertainty as a first-class citizen. It highlights what we don't know and tells us how to resolve it before release."

---

### 3:00 – Scenario Lab & Human Decision Gate
- **Action**: Toggle Scenario Lab to `EXPECTED`, then click `[ DECISION GATE ]` in Lifecycle Rail.
- **Visual**: `DecisionGate` displays 82/100 System Risk Instrument, release guardrail checklist, and decision buttons (`APPROVE`, `REVIEW`, `HOLD`).
- **Narrative**:
  > "ImpactLoop follows a strict philosophy: AI analyzes, human decides. You are the decision gate. Here we review guardrail recommendations and sign our decision."

---

### 3:45 – Closed-loop Learning & Organizational Memory
- **Action**: Switch to `OBSERVE` and `LEARN` stages in Lifecycle Rail.
- **Visual**: `OutcomePanel` shows Predicted (+4.2%) vs Observed (+2.8%) vs Delta (-1.4%), and `MemoryPanel` updates systemic model rules.
- **Narrative**:
  > "After shipping, ImpactLoop compares actual telemetry against predictions and feeds learned patterns back into organizational memory. ImpactLoop gives decision-makers full confidence before shipping."
